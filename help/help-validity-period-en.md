The valid period for time entry bounds when time can be recorded, with a From and/or To date. Entries outside the window are refused, and leaving either date empty keeps that side open-ended. Use it to close a finished project without archiving it, or to match a person's contract dates. It can be set per person, project, or tag, and the most specific value wins.
