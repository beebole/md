Localisation settings configure the time zone, first day of the week, currency, decimal format, thousands separator, clock format (12-hour or 24-hour), and date format for this entity. These preferences affect how dates, times, numbers, and currency are displayed throughout the interface. Settings are inherited from the organisation level and can be overridden per tag or person.
