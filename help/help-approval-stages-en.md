The approval workflow defines the sequential stages required to approve timesheets. Each stage can be assigned to admins, specific people, tagged people, project managers, task managers, or people managers, and its quorum sets whether any one approver or all of them must approve. Without any stage, nobody can submit a timesheet, and periods close only through Auto-submit timesheets after X days, when it is set. The workflow is inherited from the organisation level and can be overridden per person or tag.
