The approval workflow defines the sequential stages required to approve timesheets. Each stage can be assigned to admins, specific people, tagged people, project managers, task managers, or people managers, and its quorum sets whether any one approver or all of them must approve. The workflow is inherited from the organisation level and can be overridden per person or tag.
