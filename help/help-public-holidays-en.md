Public holidays define the non-working days observed by this entity. Pick a year and a country, optionally a region, and load its official holidays, then remove the ones that don't apply or add your own. These days are excluded from availability calculations and can affect absence quotas and scheduling. Holidays are inherited from the organisation level and can be customised per tag or person to reflect regional differences.
