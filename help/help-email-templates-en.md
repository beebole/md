Email templates let you customise the content of automated emails sent by the platform: sign-up and sign-in codes, invitations, mentions, submitted, approved, and rejected timesheets, reminders, approval reminders, digests, and timers stopped automatically. A template left at its default is shown in your current language and sent in each recipient's language. Templates are inherited from the organisation level and can be overridden per tag. Each template type can be edited independently.
