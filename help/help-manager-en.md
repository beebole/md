This section lists the team members, projects, tasks, and tags this person manages, and who manages this person under Managed by. Picking a whole category, such as a project category, a planning, or a tag category, makes the person manager of everything inside it, including items added later; managing a project also covers its subprojects, and managing a tag covers the people and projects tagged with it. Managers can be notified when items they manage are mentioned, and they approve timesheets when the approval workflow includes a People managers, Project managers, or Task managers stage.
