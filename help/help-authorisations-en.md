Access rights control what users assigned to this role can view and manage within the application. Most permissions have an Edit and a View level, each limited to targets such as Me, Managed people, or All, and edit access always includes view access. The Admin role (full access) checkbox grants everything at once. Assign this role to people to grant them the corresponding access across the platform.
