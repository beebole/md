Access rights control what users assigned to this role can view and manage within the application. Most permissions have an Edit and a View level, each limited to targets such as Me, Managed people, or All, and edit access always includes view access. For Tasks, the targets also include Tasks of managed people (the tasks they own or are assigned to) and Tasks on managed projects, subtasks included, and a task the role lets you view stays visible even when you can't see its owner or its project. The Admin role (full access) checkbox grants everything at once, and assigning this role to people grants them this access across the platform.
