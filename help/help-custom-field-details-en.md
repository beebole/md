Custom field details define the type and validation rules for this field. Depending on the field type (text, number, date, date & time, URL, boolean, or unique ID), you can set constraints such as min/max values, allowed values, validation patterns, placeholder text, and formatting options. A unique ID field also makes sure no two items share the same value. These rules are enforced when users fill in the field.
