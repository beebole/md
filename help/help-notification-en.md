Notification settings control which notifications this entity receives and how often. Tick Email, Push notifications, or both, then set a frequency for each type (Instant, Daily, Weekly, or None): When you're @mentioned, Items you manage are mentioned, Items assigned to you are mentioned, and Approval updates. Push notifications only reach the devices where they are enabled: in your own settings, This device shows whether the browser you are using receives them, Enable on this device turns them on there, and Other devices lists the rest. Settings are inherited from the organisation level and can be customised per tag or person.
