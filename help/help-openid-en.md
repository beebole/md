Single Sign-On lets your organisation sign in through a third-party identity provider instead of separate passwords. You can connect Google, Microsoft, or any custom OpenID Connect provider — for a custom provider, enter the issuer URL, client ID, and client secret. You can also create users automatically on first sign-in and restrict access to SSO only.
