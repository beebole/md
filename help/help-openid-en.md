Single Sign-On lets your team sign in through your organisation's identity provider. You can connect Google, Microsoft, or any custom OpenID Connect provider such as Okta — for a custom provider, enter the issuer URL, client ID, and client secret, then copy the callback URI shown into your provider's settings. With Google, you can link your domains and create users automatically on their first sign-in, and each provider can be made the only way to sign in.
