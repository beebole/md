Show or hide by default decides whether each kind of item is available to everyone in the organisation by default: projects, secondary projects, tasks, time off, expenses, schedules, and custom fields. When an option is unchecked, those items stay hidden until you grant them individually, per item under Who has access? or per tag, person, or project under Show or hide.
