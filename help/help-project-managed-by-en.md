This section shows which persons manage this project, task, or tag. Managers inherited from elsewhere, such as a parent project, show where they come from and can only be removed there. Managers can be notified when what they manage is mentioned, and they approve timesheets when the approval workflow includes a stage for them, such as Project managers or Task managers. You can assign one or more managers directly from here.
