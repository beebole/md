The owner is the single person doing this task. % FTE sets the share of their working time dedicated to it, which Beebole combines with their work schedule to fit the planned time between the task's dates, and Tentative reserves their capacity while keeping the task out of reports until it is confirmed. In a Bookings planning, the owner is the person booked, and the booking is named after its project and person.
