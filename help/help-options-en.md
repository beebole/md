Show or hide settings control which time off types, expense types, work schedules, tasks, projects, secondary projects, and custom fields are available to this person or tag; on a project, they control its secondary projects, expense types, and custom fields. By hiding the ones that don't apply, you simplify the interface so users only see the choices relevant to them. These settings help tailor the experience for different teams or individuals.
