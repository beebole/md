This section controls who has access to this entity. You can grant access to everyone, to no one, or to specific people and tags — anyone carrying a selected tag inherits access automatically. On a task, the section is called Potential owners and lists the people and tags the task is available to; on a work schedule, it is called Assigned to and shows where the schedule applies.
