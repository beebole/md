Accrual settings control how absence allowances are accumulated over time. You can enable periodic accrual with a chosen frequency and specify how many days or hours are awarded each period. Accrued time is not credited automatically yet: adjust it in the Accrued field of each allowance. The option to allow future time-off requests against accruals lets users book absences even when their accrued balance is insufficient.
