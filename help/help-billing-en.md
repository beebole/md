Billing rates define how revenue is calculated for time entries: an hourly rate, a daily rate, a fixed fee that can repeat, or non-billable. Rates can vary over time with different start dates and support multiple currencies, and an hourly or daily rate can be split by person or project. Settings are inherited from the organisation level and can be overridden per tag, person, or project to reflect different billing arrangements.
