**What it does**

Attach billable rates to your people and projects to turn tracked time into revenue.

**Why use it**

- **Flexible rates** — set them per person or project.
- **Revenue in real time** — watch billable value build up as hours are logged.
- **Confident invoicing** — bill from accurate, rate-based totals instead of guesswork.

---

Included in the **Essential** and **Advanced** plans. On tags, the **Essential** plan also needs the **Custom fields, custom roles & advanced configuration** add-on.
