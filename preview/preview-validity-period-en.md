**What it does**

You can already bound when time can be entered on each person or project. With **Advanced Tags**, you can set a valid period for time entry on everything sharing a tag, in one step.

**Why use it**

- **Close periods in bulk** — stop time entry for a whole team or client when a contract ends.
- **Cleaner timesheets** — entries outside the window are refused automatically.
- **Less busywork** — one date range instead of editing every person or project.
